# Contributing

Thank you for your interest in this technical writing portfolio! 

Because this repository serves as a professional showcase of individual technical writing examples, Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs), and governance frameworks, direct pull requests modifying core documents are generally not merged. 

However, if you spot a typo, broken link, or have constructive feedback regarding the documentation structure, please feel free to:
1. Open an **Issue** to discuss proposed improvements.
2. Fork the repository and adapt patterns for your own use (retaining proper attribution under the MIT License).

Thank you for visiting!
